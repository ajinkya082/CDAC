============================================================
CASE STUDY I – ACCOUNTS PAYABLE AND RECEIVABLES
============================================================

1. Objective
Computerise customer and supplier records, sales invoices, purchase bills, payments, receipts,
partial settlements, customer/supplier ledgers, sales/purchase analysis and cash-flow monitoring.

2. Main tables and purpose
- Customer: customer code, name, city, state, contact person, phone, fax and credit days.
- Supplier: supplier code, name, city, state, contact person, phone, fax and credit days.
- Part: part number, part name and unit rate.
- SalesInvoice: invoice header, customer, date, total, discount and tax/gross amounts.
- SalesInvoiceDetail: parts, quantities, rates and values on each invoice.
- PurchaseBill: purchase-bill header and supplier.
- PurchaseBillDetail: parts, quantities, rates and values on each bill.
- Payment: supplier payment voucher, date, amount and cheque/bank details.
- PaymentAllocation: maps a payment to one or more bills and stores amount settled.
- Receipt: customer receipt voucher and cash/cheque details.
- ReceiptAllocation: maps a receipt to one or more invoices and stores amount settled.

3. Business rules
- One invoice or bill can contain many parts.
- A supplier payment is made by cheque; one payment can settle multiple bills.
- Customer receipts can be cash or cheque; one receipt can settle multiple invoices.
- Payments and receipts may be partial.
- Discount is stored as a percentage and amount.
- Excise = 10% × (Total Amount − Discount Amount).
- Sales tax = 10% × (Total Amount − Discount Amount + Excise).
- Gross amount = Total Amount − Discount Amount + Excise + Sales Tax.
- The SQL provides customer and supplier ledger views showing billed, paid/received and outstanding amounts.
- The SQL stores invoice/bill amounts; applications should calculate and update the formulas when a
  transaction is saved. Example formula comments are included in the SQL file.

4. Reports
- Customer invoice ledger and outstanding invoice balances.
- Supplier bill ledger and outstanding bill balances.
- Payment and receipt registers (query Payment and Receipt).
- Sales and purchase analysis (aggregate SalesInvoice and PurchaseBill by customer, supplier, date or part).
- Cash-flow report (aggregate receipts and payments by date).

CASE STUDY VI – EMPLOYEE PAYROLL SYSTEM
============================================================

1. Objective
Manage permanent/temporary employees, technical/non-technical categories, salary scales, basic pay,
increments, allowances, GPF, professional tax, monthly payroll and payslips.

2. Main tables and purpose
- SalaryScale: minimum basic, increment step and maximum basic.
- Designation: designation and technical/non-technical category.
- Employee: employee code, personal data, joining date, employee type, designation, scale and joining basic.
- EmployeeIncrement: date, number of increments, old basic and new basic.
- AllowanceRate: scale-based DA percentage and HRA/CCA amounts with effective date.
- EmployeeAllowance: monthly basic, DA, HRA, CCA and arrears.
- ProfessionalTaxRate: professional-tax amount by scale and effective date.
- Payroll: monthly earnings, deductions, gross pay and net pay.

3. Business rules
- Basic salary must fall within the employee's salary scale.
- A salary scale is represented by minimum basic, increment amount and maximum basic.
- DA is calculated as a percentage of basic pay.
- HRA and CCA depend on salary scale.
- GPF = 12% of basic pay.
- Professional tax depends on scale.
- Gross pay = basic + DA + HRA + CCA + arrears.
- Total deductions = GPF + professional tax.
- Net pay = gross pay − total deductions.
- Scale/date-based rates are stored in master tables. A payroll run should select the applicable rates
  for the payroll month and store the calculated amounts in Payroll.

4. Reports
- Employee listing.
- Monthly payslips (view vw_payroll_monthly_slip).
- Scale-wise abstract report (view vw_payroll_scale_abstract).
- Allowances report from EmployeeAllowance.
- Deduction report from Payroll.
- Payroll totals by month, employee type, designation and scale.
